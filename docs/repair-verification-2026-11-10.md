This checkpoint repairs foundational generation defects. It does not establish completion of the comprehensive reconstruction and immersive-navigation roadmap.

## Changes

- Selective streaming keeps bounded useful HTML while omitting scripts and comments; explicit warnings identify truncated extraction, skipped pages and unavailable stylesheets. Download and retained-content budgets are separate. Robots policies, crawl delays, DNS validation and redirects remain enforced.
- Normalized design evidence records original image references, logos, contact links, palette, font-family names and basic layout signals. Generation consumes that evidence in preserve and redesign modes, with original assets matched to section subjects and source identity retained. Raw source scripts/styles are never executed.
- Up to four linked pages retain bounded paragraphs and page-specific image references, with cohesive responsive pages and real source links.
- Restaurant offering imagery no longer rotates arbitrary category stock onto specific products. Unknown product subjects remain text-led rather than receiving misleading photography.
- Text-bearing sections/cards remain flat. Cinematic chapter controls occupy document flow instead of covering mobile copy. Environmental and photographic depth remains available.
- The generation endpoint validates every rendered page before returning a draft; the client validates the combined site after source application.
- Panorama upload dimensions/pixel budgets adapt to device memory, with high-quality downsampling and WebGL1-safe mipmapping. This cannot restore detail absent in the original texture and does not bound original image download/decode.
- Explicit professional-services briefs can generate normally.
